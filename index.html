Mechanism Design & Precision Engineering

My approach to engineering is to build from first principles and understand systems end-to-end — designing, fabricating, testing, and iterating close to the hardware. This is the throughline across my actuator research, my Major Project, and my industry work.

I independently research a hybrid harmonic drive, and designed a 70 mm, 105.88:1 Compact High-Torque Actuator combining a planetary input stage with a 25:1 cycloidal output stage to achieve high torque and reduction density within a minimal envelope. This work is published as a paper on the compact planetary-cycloidal hybrid actuator, presented at ICASET 2026, with a journal extension currently under review.

I am currently working on my B.Tech Major Project, the Heavy-Duty Modular Swerve Drive System for Industrial and Vehicular Mobility Platforms — an omnidirectional swerve module built for 25 kg payload at FOS = 2, integrating a custom planetary gear mechanism for steering actuation with a high-torque wheel drive, along with structural layout, gear ratio selection, and bearing specification.

Industry Experience & Robotics Systems

I currently intern at a humanoid robotics company in Pune, where I design, manufacture, and test mechanical subsystems for a humanoid receptionist robot, research harmonic drives for humanoid joint torque density and backdrivability, and design a 7+ DOF anthropomorphic robotic arm using a mixed actuator architecture — cycloidal actuators at high-load proximal joints, harmonic drives at mid-joints, and custom spur gear servo motors at distal joints.

On the software side, I work with ROS 2 and reinforcement learning for locomotion and control. I'm designing the mechatronics and propulsion subsystem for an autonomous surface vessel (ASV), including hull layout, propeller drive control, and gyroscopic stabilization, with an autopilot integrated against GPS, IMU, LiDAR, and ultrasound for autonomous operation and collision avoidance. In parallel, I'm building a custom humanoid model in MuJoCo from scratch and training locomotion policies with reinforcement learning, exploring reward shaping and joint torque constraints as part of a self-directed study in physical AI.
