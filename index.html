⚙️ Mechanism Design & Precision Engineering

My approach to engineering is to build from first principles and understand systems end-to-end — designing, fabricating, testing, and iterating close to the hardware. This is the throughline across my actuator research, my Major Project, and my industry work.

I independently research a hybrid harmonic drive, combining planetary and cycloidal reduction stages into a compact, high-ratio actuator architecture. This work builds on a published paper on a compact planetary-cycloidal hybrid actuator, presented at ICASET 2026, and continues into detailed geometric design (SolidWorks/ANSYS) and FEA validation of gear tooth profiles and load paths.

I am currently working on my B.Tech Major Project, “Integrated Planetary Gearbox and Sensor Feedback Design for Heavy-Duty Mobile Robots,” which tightly couples mechanical power transmission with sensor feedback to keep high-torque robotic systems controllable, reliable, and scalable.

🤖 Industry Experience & Robotics Systems

I currently intern at a humanoid robotics company in Pune, where I work on subsystems for a humanoid receptionist robot and on a compact 106:1 high-torque actuator for humanoid joints — spanning gear design, structural sizing, and integration with sensor feedback and control.

I’m also actively expanding into Deep Learning and Vision-Language Models (VLMs), motivated by a Physical AI workshop at ROSCon 2025 that reframed how I think about simulation and control — as tools that must be grounded in real-world physics, not developed apart from it. This now informs my Quadruped Project, where simulation guides mechanical design and control architecture from the ground up.
