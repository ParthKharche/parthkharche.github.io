AI Passport | Cryptographic IP Management

Frontend Architecture & Systems Security

Sharing sensitive mechanical designs and research data often requires cumbersome NDAs and offers zero visibility into how the data is used once it leaves the source. To solve this, I developed the frontend architecture for "AI Passport," a secure Vault and Monitoring Dashboard.

This tool allows project owners to generate secure, time-sensitive access links with granular control over exactly what the recipient can view, bridging the gap between hardware engineering and digital security.

Project Deliverables & Access

  • Granular Access Control: Modular sharing capabilities that allow owners to independently toggle visibility for Abstracts, Images, 3D Models, Data, and Graphs.
  • Session Security: Integrated dynamic session watermarking that overlays the viewer's IP and timestamp onto the document viewer to deter unauthorized extraction.
