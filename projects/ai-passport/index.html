Provenance | Cryptographic IP Management & Controlled Work Disclosure

Turning uncompensated work disclosure into leverage

📄 View Project PDF 🌐 Launch HTML Prototype

Anytime someone has to show unpublished, original work before a formal agreement exists, they lose control the moment it's seen. Freelancers and engineering job candidates frequently face this problem when asked for uncompensated speculative or take-home work. Existing tools fail to close this gap: NDAs are disproportionately slow, static watermarks do not expire, and cloud drive links lack a verifiable, portable access record.

Provenance solves this by turning high-risk work disclosure into something provable and controllable. Built on AI Passport's identity layer, the system allows creators to share original work through a passkey-protected, time-boxed view where every access event is permanently logged as a cryptographically signed Audit Receipt. This ensures creators can share confidently, knowing exactly who saw what, when, and under what terms.

System Features & Deliverables

  • Granular Visibility Settings: Owners can independently toggle exactly which components are visible to the recipient, including the title, abstract, images/renders, 3D model, data, graphs, and references.
  • Cryptographic Proof: Every viewing session triggers a cryptographically signed, timestamped Audit Receipt that is visible on the owner's passport page.
  • Session Security & Revocation: Access is strictly view-only and session-watermarked with no download permissions, and can be auto-expired or manually revoked by the owner via a kill-switch.
  • Portable Context: The passport serves as a constant, trustworthy record that outlives and outranks whichever platform or messaging tool the work was originally shown on.
