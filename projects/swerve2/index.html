Heavy-Duty Modular Swerve Drive System

B.Tech Major Project | December 2025 – Present

Developing an omnidirectional swerve drive module specifically engineered for industrial and vehicular mobility platforms. The system scales up previous swerve concepts to meet severe structural and torque demands, operating within a highly constrained module envelope.

Mechanical Layout & CAD Modeling

Fig 1: Exploded view detailing the integrated planetary steering and high-torque wheel drive layout.

Load Specifications & Kinematics

  • Payload Rating: Explicitly designed and rated for a 25 kg payload at a structural Factor of Safety ($FOS=2$), ensuring reliability under industrial deployment conditions.
  • Planetary Integration: Successfully integrated a custom planetary gear mechanism specifically for steering actuation alongside a high-torque wheel drive axis.

Mechanical System Ownership

  • Component Sizing: Held direct responsibility for the complete structural layout, optimal gear ratio selection, and critical bearing specifications.
  • Packaging Constraints: Packaged both the drive and steering axes into a compact module envelope, requiring rigorous tolerance management and spatial optimization.
